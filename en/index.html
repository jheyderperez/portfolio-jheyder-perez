Jheyder Perez

Civil Engineer & Researcher

Ph.D. Student in Engineering Sciences · Pontificia Universidad Católica de Chile

Civil Engineer from Universidad San Ignacio de Loyola, with a Master's degree in Civil Engineering with a specialization in Natural Hazard Risk Management from the Pontificia Universidad Católica del Perú (PUCP), where I graduated first in my class with outstanding distinction.

I am currently a Ph.D. student in Engineering Sciences at Pontificia Universidad Católica de Chile, a Research Assistant at Tohoku University as a CDRI Fellow, and a part-time faculty member at Universidad San Ignacio de Loyola, where I advise undergraduate theses.

My research focuses on tsunami evacuation systems using agent-based models and reinforcement learning techniques, with international collaborations at Tohoku University (Japan) and Universidad Técnica Federico Santa María (Chile).

Research interests

  • 01

    Structural Assessment of Existing Buildings

    Pathology, destructive and non-destructive testing, corrosion potential and structural modeling for repair and strengthening.

  • 02

    Disaster Risk Management

    Community resilience and preparedness for earthquakes, tsunamis, debris flows and floods, with a focus on Metropolitan Lima.

  • 03

    Agent-Based Modeling (ABM)

    Simulation of pedestrian tsunami evacuation using agent-based models, with applications in Camaná, Peru, in collaboration with Tohoku University.

  • 04

    Reinforcement Learning for Evacuation

    Optimization of pedestrian evacuation routes using machine learning and reinforcement learning techniques for multi-hazard scenarios.
